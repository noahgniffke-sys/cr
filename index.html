Post one video to the accounts you sign in to. Do these three steps on this page. Nothing else is required.

  1. 1Get a key

    1. Open API Keys. Create a free account if it asks you to.
    2. Click Generate New API Key and copy it.
    3. Paste it here and press Save key. This browser keeps it.

    The free plan includes 10 posts a month. TikTok posting can require a paid plan. Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, and Threads are signed in from this page.

  2. 2Sign in

    Press Sign in on a network, log in there, and allow access. This page opens again with that network saved. Instagram has to be a Business or Creator account. Facebook has to be a Page you manage.

  3. 3Post

    Drop one video under 95 MB and write the caption. Post to all sends it to every network you signed in to. Post on one row sends it only there. Story on Instagram or Facebook posts a story that lasts 24 hours. Choose Schedule to send it later. When this page says it was sent, you can close the tab.
